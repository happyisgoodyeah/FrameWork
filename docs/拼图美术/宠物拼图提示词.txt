常规体型

将下方共用提示与对应宠物的两行描述拼接，使用指定原图作笔触和配色参考。启用透明背景。

Use case: style-transfer. Transform the supplied single old Unity pet sprite into a four-frame animation asset for another puzzle pet in the same game's cast. Input image is the art-direction reference, preserve its particular handmade simplicity and stroke scale while redrawing the subject and pose. Output ONE square transparent PNG 2 by 2 sprite sheet for ONE character, four separated near-identical drawings facing the same direction and in exactly the same pose, one centered in each equal quadrant. Exactly 2 columns and 2 rows, no grid lines. Size the character to use 82–88% of the available width OR height of each quadrant, same scale and position in every quadrant, good clear transparent gutters, no touching. Four subtle animation keyframes: top left normal pose, top right a relaxed blink or breath, bottom left a surprised lifted expression, bottom right the same lifted pose settling, subtle ear, tail or paw motion. Keep silhouettes and poses anatomically consistent; each quadrants' footprint stays the same. Use the same imperfect hand-drawn game sprite style as supplied, very few wobbly near-black dry felt-brush strokes with big irregularities in width, breaks and ink skips; no fine hatching, hairline pencilling, fur texture or vector contours. Opaque flat low-saturation pastel blocks, warm cream, a couple of blunt white dry-brush highlights, tiny dusty coral cheek dashes, scarcely any shadow, if needed one muted grey-purple mark. Ink eyes like vertical seeds or closed arcs, ample breathing room in unadorned color areas, very simple adorable expression and mild asymmetry. A quick naïve scribble in a basic painting program, not polished sticker merchandise. Minimal small scale detail. Keep background actually transparent RGBA throughout and in all negative spaces, no surrounding white keyline, background, paper, ground, labels, captions, letters, diagram or floating marks. The imaginary grid describes the animal's silhouette only; never draw cells, lines, cubes or a tile behind it.

竖尾的长 L 形与 T 形

参考图一为三列两行的占格图。上排占左格、下排占满三格的是暹罗猫。上排占中格、下排占满三格的是布偶猫。占格填灰绿，空格填浅灰。参考图二使用 cat/00.png。两种造型都把身体压低，以竖起的尾巴形成突出的一格。将 {POSE} 替换成相应姿势。

Draw a transparent 2 by 2 sprite sheet for ONE animal, four separated full bodies, same side view, flat on the belly, exactly the same silhouette each time. In EACH quadrant reproduce the proportions of the green footprint from picture 1. Pale grey regions in the guide must end up empty ALPHA. Never draw a square, template, patch of green, frame or caption. Picture 2 is solely the established linework/brush style for the little animal. {POSE} Keep the whole animal big in each frame and safely inside, with transparent gutters. TL alert, TR blink, BL surprised mouth/tail twitch, BR relax while held. Breed and geometry more important than embellishment. Tangibly irregular near-black broken blunt brush outlines, coarse imperfect strokes with varying thickness, the small vertical grain eyes, coral blush dashes, coarse white ink skips, a couple of opaque low-saturation blush/cream/grey-purple flat colors. Crude fast hand-painted game doodle, minimal lines, no hair texture, shadow, white sticker line or floor. Genuinely transparent RGBA.
